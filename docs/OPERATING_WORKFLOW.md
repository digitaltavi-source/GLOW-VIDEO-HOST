# Operating workflow

1. Receive a real customer brief, objectives, audience, references, constraints and rights information. Preserve unknowns rather than fabricate them.
2. Prepare H1 truth and creative intent for exact operator review/acceptance.
3. Prepare H2 detailed shot/creative plan and H3 capability/material/budget/readiness plan, with controlled acceptance of each.
4. Export the frozen external MCP work package. The user executes it through the connected generation provider, such as Magnific, in their authorized account.
5. Record outputs with evidence and explicitly review them. Claimed output URI/hash alone does not prove independently verified media bytes.
6. Obtain customer review and assess actual visual/audio/continuity/rights quality before delivery.

Intake/H1 preparation and restart persistence are demonstrated live; later live gates remain pending. Synthetic readiness, operator review, customer approval and professional release are distinct states.

Repository organization preserves existing research and evidence; it does not itself certify creative quality or a price tier.
